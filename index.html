Trillion, our personal publishing assistant

Stokbread makes short vertical video clips. Trillion is the small piece of software on our own computer that publishes those clips to our own accounts on YouTube, Instagram, Facebook and TikTok, at fixed times.

Our own content

Trillion only publishes clips we made ourselves, from sources that allow reuse or gave permission, with credit to the creator.

Our own accounts

It posts only to accounts owned by Stokbread. It is not a public service and no other people sign in to it.

Checked before posting

Every clip passes checks first: reuse licence or permission, length, credit to the creator, no sensitive topics. We approve it ourselves, or the checks do after a waiting time.

How it works

  1. We sign in once on each platform with the official login and allow Trillion to upload videos to our account.
  2. New clips wait for our review; a clip that passes every check is approved automatically after a few hours, the others wait for us.
  3. At each scheduled time Trillion uploads the next approved clip, with its caption, through the platform's official API.

Platforms and data

Trillion uses the official APIs of YouTube (YouTube Data API), Instagram and Facebook (Meta Graph API) and TikTok (Content Posting API). The access tokens stay on our own computer. Read the privacy policy and the terms of use.
