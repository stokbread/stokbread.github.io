Privacy policy

Last updated: 4 October 2026

This policy explains what the application Trillion ("the app") does with data. The app is operated by Stokbread (Ayoub), Rotterdam, the Netherlands, contact ytclipsayoub@gmail.com. The app is a private tool: it runs on our own computer and only publishes our own video clips to social media accounts owned by Stokbread. It is not offered to the public and no other people sign in to it.

1. What data the app uses

  • Access tokens that YouTube (Google), Instagram and Facebook (Meta) and TikTok issue when we sign in to our own accounts with their official login and allow the app to upload videos.
  • Basic account information of our own accounts that these platforms return, such as the account or page ID and name, needed to know where to upload.
  • Our own videos and captions, which the app uploads, and the IDs the platforms return for the published posts.

The app does not collect data about other people, followers or viewers. It does not read messages, comments or contacts.

2. What we use it for

Only to publish our own approved clips to our own accounts at scheduled times, and to keep a record of what was published where.

3. Where it is stored and who sees it

  • Tokens and records are stored only on our own computer, in local files that are not shared.
  • We do not sell, rent or share data with anyone. Data only goes to the platform it belongs to, through that platform's official API.
  • We do not use the data for advertising or profiling, and we do not use it to train AI models.

4. Google user data

The app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The app uses the YouTube Data API only to upload videos to our own YouTube channel and to read basic information about that channel. See also the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

5. Meta and TikTok

The app uses the Meta Graph API and the TikTok Content Posting API only to publish our own videos to our own Instagram account, Facebook Page and TikTok account, in line with the Meta Platform Terms and the TikTok Developer Terms of Service.

6. How long we keep it

Tokens are kept as long as the connection is in use. Records of published posts are kept as our own administration. You can revoke the app's access at any time in the security settings of the platform (Google: security.google.com/settings/security/permissions, Meta "Business integrations", TikTok "Manage app permissions"); the tokens then stop working.

7. Your rights

If you believe the app processes data about you, you can ask us what we hold and ask us to delete it, by emailing ytclipsayoub@gmail.com. You may also lodge a complaint with the Dutch data protection authority (Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens).

8. Changes

We will update this page when the app changes how it handles data, with a new date at the top.
